# High-Conversion Web3 Landing Page Skill

Use this skill when designing, building, or refining the public-facing landing page (pndigi.site / pp/page.tsx). A Web3 launchpad landing page must balance cryptographic credibility, excitement, and effortless conversion.

## 1. Anatomy of a Top-Tier Web3 Landing Page
1. **Hero Section (The Hook):**
   - **Headline:** Punchy value proposition (e.g., *"The Fair-Launch Social Liquidity Engine on Base"*).
   - **Sub-headline:** Clear explanation without jargon (e.g., *"Launch tokens with automated bonding curves, partner with verified KOL syndicates, and trade with zero rugpull risk."*).
   - **Dual CTAs:**
     - Primary: *"Launch Your Token"* (Glow gradient button pointing to /launchpad).
     - Secondary: *"Explore Pools & Deal Rooms"* (Ghost border button pointing to /kol or /trading).
   - **Hero Visual:** Live interactive bonding curve simulation or 3D floating token badge with live dynamic tickers.
2. **Social Proof & Live Stats Strip (Trust Building):**
   - Real-time or verifiable counters: Total Value Locked, Active Pools, Verified KOLs, Zero Rugpull Guarantee.
   - Partner / Network Badges: Powered by Base, Uniswap V3, EIP-4361 SIWE.
3. **Core Feature Grid (Interactive Bento Box):**
   - Card 1: *Autonomous Bonding Curve* (Dynamic formula visualization).
   - Card 2: *KOL Angel Deal Room* (Social verification & Sybil resistance).
   - Card 3: *Deterministic Risk Gate* (Non-custodial, MEV-protected execution).
   - Card 4: *Multi-Chain Liquidity Routing* (Instant quotes across Base, Solana, and EVM).
4. **How It Works (3-Step Simplicity):**
   - Step 1: Connect Wallet (One-click SIWE with MetaMask).
   - Step 2: Set Curve Parameters or Join a Pool.
   - Step 3: Trade, Syndicate, or Graduate to Uniswap.
5. **Security & Audit Section:**
   - Highlights: Non-custodial contracts, Fail-closed risk gates, 100% On-chain settlement.
6. **Footer & Ecosystem Links:**
   - Network status (Base Sepolia / Mainnet badge), documentation links, Twitter/X, Telegram, and contract addresses.
